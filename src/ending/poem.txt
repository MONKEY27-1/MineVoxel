[[ Format: each line is prefixed with A: or B: to mark the speaker.     ]]
[[ Render the two voices in distinct colors. Blank lines are beats —    ]]
[[ hold roughly two seconds on each. Strip [[ ]] lines before display.  ]]
[[ Voice note: A wonders and asks. B answers flat and dry. Keep them    ]]
[[ distinct — the poem stops working if they blur together.             ]]
[[ Target scroll time: ~8 minutes. Tune per-line delay, not scroll      ]]
[[ speed, and let the beats hold.                                       ]]

A: They have stopped.

B: I know.

A: At the edge of the light.

B: Looking out.

A: Looking back.

B: Both, somehow.


A: Do you think they know this is the end?

B: No.

A: How can you be certain?

B: Because they are still waiting for something to happen.


A: Something always did.

B: A morning.

A: A storm.

B: A cave opening under their feet.

A: A place they had not seen yet.

B: There was always another hill.

A: Not now.

B: No.

B: Now there is only the gate.


A: We have watched them a long time.

B: Longer than they know.

A: We watched them learn to walk.

B: We watched them learn to look up.

A: We watched them stop being afraid of the first night.

B: And start being afraid of different things.

A: At first, the dark.

B: Later, the long fall.

A: Later still, losing what they had made.

B: And at the very end —

A: That there might be nothing left to make.


B: Do you remember the first house?

A: The little one.

B: Dirt walls.

A: One torch.

B: No windows.

A: They called it home anyway.

B: It was ugly.

A: It was beautiful.

B: Those are not the same thing.

A: They are to the person who built it.


A: They slept there with the rain outside.

B: They woke up and opened the door.

A: They had no idea where they were going.

B: They went anyway.


A: Then they wandered.

B: Constantly.

A: They crossed rivers that did not need crossing.

B: Climbed mountains with nothing at the top.

A: Walked into caves because a dark opening looked interesting.

B: Dug down because there might be something under the next layer.

A: And under that one.

B: And under that one.

A: They kept looking.

B: Even when looking cost them.


A: They carried too much.

B: Always.

A: Wood they did not need. Stone they would never place.

B: Seeds. Flowers they never planted.

A: A map to somewhere they could not find twice.

B: Why keep it?

A: Because it had mattered once.

B: That is not a reason.

A: Neither is any memory.


A: They built a tower.

B: Yes.

A: Nobody needed it.

B: Nobody lived in it.

A: It stood over the trees with a light at the top.

B: They climbed it every evening.

A: Just to look.

B: At the world?

A: At the fact that they had changed it.


B: They made paths where there had been none.

A: Bridges where there had been water.

B: Doors where there had been walls.

A: Fires where there had only been dark.


A: And sometimes they broke it all apart.

B: The roof was wrong.

A: The floor was wrong.

B: The room was too small, and then too large.

A: So they tore it down.

B: And built it again.

A: Six times, that one room.

B: They thought that was failing.

A: Was it?

B: It is the opposite. You do not rebuild a thing you have stopped caring about.


A: They learned the dark slowly.

B: First they hid from it.

A: Then they carried a light into it.

B: Then they went further in than the light could reach.

A: Leaving torches behind them.

B: Little pieces of daylight. One at a time.

A: So they could find the way back.

B: So there would be a way back to find.


A: There are tunnels under this world nobody will ever walk again.

B: They are still lit.

A: Does that matter, if no one sees them?

B: Especially then.


B: They went to the burning place.

A: Through a door they made out of black stone and set on fire.

B: Everything there wanted them dead.

A: They came back with their arms full.

B: They always came back with their arms full.


A: They died.

B: Often.

A: Stone fell on them. They misjudged a jump in the dark.

B: They trusted the wrong silence.

A: They burned. They drowned.

B: They fell a very long way once, without making a sound.

A: And every time, the world gathered them up and set them down at the beginning.

B: As if it had only ever wanted to say: try again.

A: And they did.

B: Every time.


A: Do you remember when they stopped running from everything?

B: Yes.

A: They still ran.

B: Of course.

A: But sometimes they ran toward it.

B: That was the change.

A: Into what?

B: Someone who could choose.


A: And at the end they came here.

B: The last island. The pale one. The one with nothing underneath.

A: They broke the crystals while it circled.

B: They stood in the open with no roof and no walls and waited for it to come back around.

A: They were not brave.

B: No.

A: They were frightened, and they stayed.

B: Which is the only kind of brave there has ever been.


B: They are still at the gate.

A: They can see it.

B: They are listening.

A: Can they hear us?

B: They always could.

A: Then we should stop talking about them.

B: And start talking to them.


A: You.

B: Yes. You.


A: Look around.

B: Not at the gate.

A: At the room you are actually sitting in.

B: There are no square mountains outside that window.

A: No ocean that goes on forever.

B: No sun that rises exactly when you expect it.

A: No path telling you which way is home.

B: And still you know where you are.


A: Your hands are near you.

B: They have been there the whole time.

A: Move one. Just the fingers.

B: There.

A: Your shoulders are higher than they need to be.

B: They have been for hours.

A: Let them down.

B: It is later than you think.

A: It usually is.


A: Here, nothing happens where you are not looking.

B: The far islands are folded shut. The deep caves are unlit and unshaped.

A: This world waited for you, perfectly, because waiting is all it can do.


B: That is not true of the other one.

A: No.

B: Right now, rain is falling on a street you are not standing in.

A: Bread is rising in a room you have never entered.

B: Someone you love is doing something ordinary at this exact moment.

A: Nobody is watching them either.

B: And it is happening anyway.


A: That is the difference.

B: Here, you were necessary.

A: There, you are wanted.

B: It is a better thing to be.


A: You were never really lost in here.

B: Numbers in the corner of your eye. A needle that pointed home.

A: A bed you could return to, and a morning waiting on the other side of it.

B: Out there, you will not always know.

A: You will take a wrong turn and it will cost you something you cannot walk back and pick up off the ground.


A: We are not going to tell you it is easy.

B: It is not. It was never going to be.

A: But you have done all of this before.


A: You know how to start with almost nothing.

B: A little wood. A little stone.

A: One wall, and then another.

B: You know how to walk into the dark with a light in your hand.

A: You know how to leave a trail behind you so you can find your way out.

B: You know how to tear down the roof and make it again, because you wanted to.

A: You have been practicing.


B: There will be days out there when everything you built looks small.

A: What do they do then?

B: Stand further back.

A: Why?

B: Sometimes you have to leave the house before you can see that it was a home.


A: And the days when the dark is bigger than the light?

B: Make another light.

A: And if that one goes out?

B: Make another.

A: You say it like it is simple.

B: It is simple. I did not say it was easy.


A: They thought they were looking for an ending.

B: They were learning how to keep going.


A: So stand up.

B: Slowly. You have been sitting a long while.

A: Drink something. You forgot again.

B: Open the window.

A: Listen to whatever is out there.

B: A car. The wind. A door somewhere in the building.

A: Someone laughing in another room.

B: The world going on without asking your permission.


A: And when you go outside — and you should go outside —

B: — look up.

A: There is a sky over there too.

B: We are told it is very good.


A: This place will keep.

B: The tower will stand on the ridge with its light on.

A: The path will go quiet under the grass.

B: The torches will burn in the tunnels, in the dark, for no one.

A: Nothing will rot. Nothing will be taken. Nobody will move your things.


A: And when it gets dark again, and you want somewhere to put your hands —

B: — come back.

A: It will be night when you arrive.

B: It always is.

A: You will be afraid of it for about a minute.

B: And then you will do what you always do.

A: Make a light.

B: Then take one step.

A: Then another.

B: Until you are somewhere you have never been.

A: And then?

B: Build there.


A: Goodbye.

B: Not goodbye.

A: No.

A: Not goodbye.


B: Goodnight.
